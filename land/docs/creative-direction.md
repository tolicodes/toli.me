# Toli Land — a playful symbol atlas

Toli Land is a collection of recognizable objects and characters drawn in dark hand-inked cartoon outlines and soft watercolor on warm cream paper. Large silhouettes and generous empty space make the things Toli creates and cares about easy to recognize.

## A crown at the center

A golden crown marks the Capital. On the world map, a few thin pale-gold paths connect it to the seven category symbols: a laptop, living book, microphone, palette, bicycle, treasure chest, and quill with inkwell. The Capital map gathers four featured project symbols around the same crown. Capital and category landmarks lead to the same project record and story. Featured choices remain easy to change.

Keep paths sparse and subordinate to the objects. The maps have no city detail, buildings, crowds, landscape scenery, ornate signs, or dense road networks. Small watercolor ground shadows and occasional grass strokes can anchor the symbols without filling the cream background.

## Recognizable objects and characters

- PickleJS: plain Pickle Rick, with a bumpy green body, pale-blue unibrow, half-closed eyes, and an open grin.
- Frontend Infra Book: a smiling green book with gold corners, paper arms, ribbon legs, and brown boots. Keep this identity consistent across the world, Capital, Publications, and detail artwork. Its project page has short chapters.
- NYC LeetCode Squad: a red apple between golden code braces.
- Easter Creatures: a cracked pastel egg with blue, pink, and yellow creatures peeking out.
- API Scraping: a browser window, a net, and colorful data cubes.
- Speaking: microphones, speech bubbles, a smiling laptop with headphones, and a pair of interview chairs.
- Writing: a heart lantern, intertwined ribbons, weather-filled glasses, a mended heart, an hourglass, and a Dota mage in purple-and-gold robes with three glowing spell orbs.
- Creative: the egg creatures, a paint palette with brushes, and a steaming clawfoot tub.
- Hobbies: literal objects and small figures representing each activity, with the same uncluttered treatment.
- Misc: individual curiosities represented by simple objects. Mature content keeps the existing interface notices.

Use generated raster artwork for the illustrations and the existing icon library for interface controls. Project detail art uses the same subject, palette, and character identity as its map symbol. Faces, hands, feet, and defining props stay visible at ordinary zoom and in phone crops.

## Labels and exploration

Names are persistent HTML labels below the illustrated symbols, separate from the raster artwork. Keep them readable at the normal map view and available without hovering. Motif descriptions describe the visible object succinctly; project facts and explanations remain in the existing content records.

Drag to explore, pinch or use controls to zoom, and tap a symbol or its label to enter. Keyboard users can tab through landmarks and use map controls. A compact atlas provides direct travel and searchable access to every item. Returning restores the previous map view. Reduced-motion preferences remove camera flights and decorative movement.

## Scope

A complete frontend using the public site's existing content and links, generated artwork, project explanations, responsive navigation, and accessible alternatives to map exploration. No new account, analytics, or backend is required.
